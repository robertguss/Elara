## Outcome

After the committed v2 future drand round is available, verify it and deterministically rematerialize every seed-bound v2 confirmatory input. Produce new identities and digests; do not reuse v1 artifacts as v2 evidence.

## Scope

* Fetch and cryptographically verify the exact committed future beacon.
* Regenerate corpus membership/order, row selection, generated literals, fixture commits/workspace digests, manifest, and all version/checksum fields from the v2 seed.
* Regenerate adapter-equivalence fixtures without executing confirmatory fault rows.
* Revalidate pinned Lemon's unchanged source capabilities and create a version-aligned zero-external-fault / Insufficient-comparability attestation. If comparator or adapter semantics differ, stop and create a separate reviewed comparability unit.
* Regenerate the dogfood plan version, seed-derived execution order, assignments, target/config identity, and checksums. Historical membership may remain 12 tasks only when the frozen v2 rule selects it.
* Prove deterministic replay from beacon to every generated artifact.

## Non-goals

No internal confirmatory fault execution, dogfood execution, target tuning, membership substitution, comparator modification, or manual generated-value edits.

## Acceptance criteria

All artifacts identify v2 and the exact beacon; replay is byte-identical; v1 artifacts remain preserved; every task/row has immutable fixture and ground-truth identity; external floor disposition is version-aligned; dogfood P/I/C and order are explicit; zero relevant v2 fault outputs are produced.
