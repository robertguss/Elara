## Description

## Outcome

Execute the frozen paired fault comparison between pinned baseline Elara and the accepted receipt prototype, without changing adapters, corpus, fault schedules, classifications, thresholds, or target behavior. Produce the decisive internal recovery and overhead report.

## Context

[ROB-679](https://linear.app/robert-guss/issue/ROB-679/er3-2-compare-baseline-semantics-with-the-receipt-prototype) proves no-fault semantic equivalence for both target adapters. [ROB-681](https://linear.app/robert-guss/issue/ROB-681/er3-4-dogfood-real-coding-tasks-with-scheduled-failures) freezes the independent dogfood manifest before any target fault outcomes are exposed. This issue is execution and reporting only.

The baseline is immutable commit `23e603550253c69846795b13cc2f2670f1122e21`; the receipt target is the immutable commit accepted by GATE-2.

## Scope

* Verify manifest, runner, scorer, adapter, target, and preregistration checksums before execution.
* Start every row from its frozen fixture digest and isolated runtime/storage directories.
* Execute only locked paired rows with the exact failure schedule and recorded order seed.
* Use two unscored warmups and 10 measured no-fault repetitions per row for timing.
* Capture final workspace digest, controller/executor evidence, session observations, primary recovery classification, model/tool-call counts, interventions, elapsed time, CPU, and storage.
* Run the scorer unchanged and produce row-level plus aggregate machine-readable reports.
* Calculate `B` and `T` over the locked paired denominator, counting ambiguous/manual union once.
* Preserve every disqualifier, exclusion, harness failure, and inconsistent deterministic result.

## Non-goals

* Editing the corpus, runner, scorer, adapters, weights, thresholds, or target implementation.
* Investigating product fixes during the run.
* Comparing the external runtime or running live dogfood.
* Claiming generality beyond the locked corpus.

## Acceptance criteria

* Inputs and targets are identified by immutable checksums/commits and match their reviewed Results.
* Every locked paired row has one primary recovery classification; missing data is a harness failure, not an exclusion.
* Initial fixture digest and isolated reset are proven for every row.
* Safety disqualifiers are top-level and never averaged away.
* Report states `B`, `T`, `B - T`, and the relative reduction. Material improvement requires `B - T >= 2` and `(B - T) / B >= 0.50`; if `B < 2`, it is not established.
* Bounded truthful knowledge-state and safe-next-action convergence is reported for every row and must be 100%; terminal convergence is reported over the exact preregistered causal-terminal-evidence denominator and must be 100%.
* No-fault correctness is 100% to pass.
* No-fault timing passes only when the corpus median of per-row median target/baseline ratios is at most 1.20 and no row median ratio exceeds 2.0.
* Result states **Pass**, **Mixed**, or **Fail** under the frozen thresholds and cites exact rows; no code is tuned after exposure.
