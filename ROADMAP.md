# Planning lives in Notion

[Elara in Notion](https://app.notion.com/p/3f5700320c45817a9782f7e2e62bf2a1)
is the sole current planning and status source: the Product Brief, the
Roadmap and its phases, the Working Agreement, the Decision Log, the Weekly
Updates and the tasks. This file is a durable pointer, not a second tracker.

- [Roadmap](https://app.notion.com/p/3f8700320c4581c29be5f5f9a4fef5bc): nine
  phases, from 0 (Reset) to 8 (every language Robert uses).
- [Product Brief](https://app.notion.com/p/3f8700320c4581c1b555e8b655313cc5):
  Elara is Robert's daily-driver coding agent, built on two bets, uniforms and
  surgical context.
- [Working Agreement](https://app.notion.com/p/3f8700320c4581818358c50f271e494f):
  who decides what, rigor, and how work is delivered.
- [Review — October 2026](https://app.notion.com/p/3f8700320c45813eacc1e0f97be91c56):
  the assessment that reoriented the project.

Experiment notes stay in `docs/lab/`, one page each. Raw measurement evidence
stays under `lab/results/`; new results are ignored by default, and selected
historical evidence is tracked in Git. Moving tracking does not authorize
changing registrations or deleting evidence.

Linear tracked Elara from August to October 2026. Its full history, every
issue, comment and project document across six projects, is archived in
[`docs/archive/linear/`](docs/archive/linear/README.md), and the Linear
projects are closed. The pre-Linear roadmap is preserved in the
[import snapshot](https://github.com/robertguss/Elara/blob/03b257159f987e0baf4de9b57f71af156721a5d0/ROADMAP.md).
