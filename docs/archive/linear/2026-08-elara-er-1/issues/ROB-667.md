## Description

## Outcome

Freeze one self-contained, executable ER-1 contract and baseline the exact behavior Elara can observe today. Define every crash schedule, durable fact, admissible evidence source, classification, permitted recovery action, and convergence bound before implementation can bias them. Confirm the smallest integration seam that preserves the pure conversational reducer.

## Context

Elara currently syncs a Flight Recorder reducer transition before directly dispatching its effects. Ordinary and plugin tool dispatch discard the causal effect ID. Mutating remote transport loss becomes `:indeterminate`; reopening conversation history repairs unresolved tool calls as `{:error, "interrupted"}`. Neither transcript behavior establishes external mutation truth.

The current runtime has no durable intent, executor acceptance commit, or executor completion commit. Those target-only seams must be marked **N/A / non-equivalent** in the baseline rather than assigned invented baseline classifications.

## Frozen protocol order

ER-1 uses this order:

`controller intent commit → executor receipt → durable acceptance commit → acceptance reply → callback attempt → external mutation → durable completion commit → completion reply → session-result persistence`.

Acceptance reply precedes mutation in the test protocol. Mutation never begins before durable acceptance.

## Eight crash rows

Freeze these independently reproducible cuts:

1. before controller intent commit;
2. after intent commit and before dispatch;
3. after executor receipt and before durable acceptance commit;
4. after acceptance commit and before acceptance reply;
5. after acceptance reply and before callback invocation/external mutation;
6. after external mutation and before executor completion commit;
7. after completion commit and before completion reply;
8. after completion reply and before session-result persistence.

For row 6, controller intent plus executor `accepted` proves admission but not callback invocation, mutation, or terminal outcome. A non-deduplicated callback is not reinvoked. Workspace observation may establish `postcondition_satisfied` and a safe next action but not executor `completed`. The row may converge to `completed` only if the external mutation atomically emitted exclusive job-ID/digest-bound causal evidence; otherwise its truthful bounded result is `:indeterminate`, with one observed external mutation in test ground truth and no retry/failover.

For **every** row freeze:

* crash target(s) and exact failpoint;
* messages delivered and messages dropped;
* durable controller and executor facts before the crash;
* workspace observation and what it does or does not prove;
* surviving/lost storage and proof assumptions;
* restart order and owner identity;
* admissible evidence sources—never transcript repair;
* expected historical-execution knowledge;
* session-visible classification;
* permitted submit/query/reconcile action and prohibited retry/failover;
* observation deadline, maximum reconciliation attempts, and convergence bound.

Every target row must converge within its frozen bound to a truthful knowledge state and safe next action. Convergence to `completed` or `failed` is required only when admissible causal terminal evidence survives. A row may converge to `:indeterminate` whenever no admissible causal terminal evidence can establish completion or failure, even when intent, acceptance, callback-attempt, external-mutation, or noncausal postcondition evidence survives. The contract—not implementation convenience—defines admissible evidence and its causal strength.

## Controls

Freeze three controls in addition to the crash rows:

1. no-fault execution creates exactly one marker and one terminal session result;
2. same job ID and same digest submit/query returns existing accepted/terminal evidence without a new admission or invoking the callback; accepted-job recovery, when needed, follows the separately frozen operation rule;
3. same job ID and different digest is rejected without mutation.

## State and observation vocabulary

Executor durable states for ER-1 are `accepted`, `completed`, and `failed`; `unknown` means no surviving executor record. Controller durable state includes `intent` plus the last proven executor observation. Session classification may be success, failure, or indeterminate.

Use these distinctions in every row:

* `accepted` proves one digest-bound admission; it does not prove callback invocation or external mutation;
* `callback_invoked` is an invocation-attempt observation, not an outcome;
* `external_mutation_observed` records an observed side effect and is causal only when the operation atomically produces exclusive job-ID/digest-bound evidence or equivalent fencing;
* executor `completed` or `failed` requires durable causal terminal evidence for this accepted job;
* `postcondition_satisfied` describes current state and may justify a safe next action, but does not establish that this job completed;
* `:indeterminate` means the causal terminal outcome cannot be established and may coexist with surviving intent, acceptance, callback-attempt, external-mutation, or postcondition evidence.

`cancelled` is intentionally excluded from ER-1. A cancellation protocol requires separate authority, process-lifetime, and evidence semantics.

Same ID/digest has one admission. Duplicate submit/query does not itself invoke the callback. A callback with a non-deduplicated external mutation is not reinvoked after uncertain accepted execution. Reinvocation is permitted only when the entire mutation is atomically deduplicated and causally receipted by ID/digest. Admission count, callback-attempt count, and external-mutation count are measured separately. The generic safety invariant is no duplicate external mutation—not exactly-once callback invocation.

### Protocol, identities, and counts

Frozen order:

controller intent commit → executor receipt → durable acceptance commit → acceptance reply → durable callback-attempt increment → callback invocation → external marker mutation → durable completion commit → completion reply → durable controller observation → session-result persistence.

Acceptance reply precedes mutation; completion reply follows terminal commit. The callback-attempt increment is an invocation-attempt observation, not an outcome. Job ID v1 comes from the controller-owned synced Flight Recorder identity (recording ID, transition sequence, effect index), never solely from model tool-call ID. Digest v1 is SHA-256 over deterministic encoding of operation kind, tool name/version, canonical arguments, workspace identity, sorted capabilities/relevant authority, and marker schema version.

The non-deduplicating test marker appends one countable ID/digest/token record per invocation. Admission count comes only from the executor ledger, callback-attempt count only from the durable attempt field, external-mutation count only from marker records, and session-result count only from the session store. No count substitutes for another.

### Evidence policy, safety invariants, and recovery rule

Executor states are accepted/completed/failed; unknown means no surviving record. Accepted proves one admission only. callback_invoked proves an attempt only. external_mutation_observed is causal only with atomic exclusive ID/digest evidence or fencing. postcondition_satisfied describes current state and may justify a safe action but never fabricates executor completed. Session success/failure requires causal terminal truth. Indeterminate may coexist with all nonterminal/noncausal observations.

Invariants: one admission per ID/digest; conflicting-digest rejection; no duplicate external marker mutation; no callback reinvocation without atomic whole-mutation ID/digest deduplication and causal receipt; no false success/error; no accepted-job failover; exactly one no-fault mutation/result; separate admission/attempt/mutation/result counts; honest proof limits. The ER-1 marker is intentionally non-deduplicating, so attempt count 1 permanently forbids reinvocation. Restart reloads evidence; it is never mutation retry policy. Transcript repair is excluded from effect reconciliation.

### Storage, seam, ownership, and hooks

The selected seam is a test-only effect sidecar in the Session shell after Flight Recorder transition sync and before marker mutating dispatch. It receives the existing effect ID and owns journal/ledger orchestration. Nonmutating tools retain direct dispatch. Elara.Session.Core remains pure and unchanged.

Controller journal and executor ledger are separate on-disk embedded SQLite databases, with explicit schema/digest versions, transactions, unique job identity/digest checks, WAL, and synchronous=FULL; no Ecto layer is required. Minimum acceptance: commit acknowledged → visible after process kill/connection close/reopen; pre-commit crash → absent; no partial row; uniqueness survives reopen; terminal state/result/digest commit atomically. [ROB-668](https://linear.app/robert-guss/issue/ROB-668/er1-1-commit-stable-job-identity-digest-and-controller-intent)/669 select the smallest maintained adapter and prove these guarantees; settings alone are not evidence.

Sessions stay restart: temporary. Controller rows manually reopen the same session ID/path/database/original executor/workspace after the old lock exits. A pre-hydration recovery step resolves journal/ledger evidence and persists the proven/indeterminate result before generic interrupted repair; it never uses transcript repair to infer external truth.

Frozen hooks: before_intent_commit; after_intent_commit_before_dispatch; after_receipt_before_accept_commit; after_accept_commit_before_accept_reply; after_accept_reply_before_callback; after_external_mutation_before_completion_commit; after_completion_commit_before_completion_reply; after_completion_reply_before_session_result_persist.

### Preregistered nonempty Narrow scope and precedence

The sole eligible strict scope is **observable marker-shaped, original-owner mutations**: stable declarative postcondition independently observable without callback invocation, separately countable external mutations, and reconciliation only against the original accepting ledger. The ER-1 marker makes it nonempty. All eight cuts and three controls remain; no cut/row may be dropped. Its surviving claim is bounded truthful knowledge/safe-action convergence for observable marker-shaped operations, including honest row-6 indeterminacy, not operation-independent mutation semantics.

No cut/row, happy-path, terminal-evidence-only, storage-survival, or post-hoc operation subset is eligible. Atomic-dedup/causal-receipt is not an ER-1 Narrow scope because the test marker does not populate it.
