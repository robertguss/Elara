## Description

## Outcome

Implement and validate a target-neutral manifest loader, fixture reset, row runner, evidence schema, and frozen scorer against the materialized corpus. Prove deliberate good/bad fixtures classify correctly before target fault evidence exists.

## Canonical references

* [ROB-772 preregistration](<https://linear.app/robert-guss/issue/ROB-772/fnd-2-preregister-evidence-thresholds-and-comparator-suitability>)
* [Frozen EXP-003 artifact](<https://github.com/robertguss/elixir-harness/blob/cd7b5fe65cc3fc073c51c86a56b5dc7fcbea9054/docs/experiments/003-effect-receipt-confirmatory-preregistration.md>)

## Scope

* Validate versioned manifests/checksums and deterministically create/reset fixtures.
* Implement explicit target-adapter and fault-hook boundaries without target-specific recovery semantics.
* Capture initial/final workspace, causal/noncausal operation observations, classifications, interventions, timing, CPU, storage, and harness failures.
* Implement [ROB-772](https://linear.app/robert-guss/issue/ROB-772/fnd-2-preregister-evidence-thresholds-and-comparator-suitability)'s primary recovery rubric, paired denominator, bad-recovery predicate, B/T formulas, disqualifiers, and missing/non-comparable/harness-failure distinctions exactly.
* Represent two warmups plus ten measured no-fault repetitions without scoring warmups and three fixed-interleaving repetitions per fault condition.
* Add deliberately good/bad/invalid fixtures for all frozen classes and dry-run every no-fault corpus fixture through a neutral fixture adapter only.

## Non-goals

No baseline/receipt/Lemon adapters; no confirmatory target fault rows; no frozen-input changes or target optimization.

## Acceptance criteria

* Runner/scorer consume only immutable, versioned target-neutral inputs and adapter responses.
* Fixture reset reproduces every initial digest across repeats.
* Scorer counts the bad-recovery union once and never offsets a safety disqualifier with aggregate value.
* Missing, non-comparable, skipped, inconsistent, and harness-failure rows remain distinct.
* Deliberate fixtures receive exact expected results and at least two invalid inputs fail with actionable diagnostics.
* No target/comparator confirmatory fault result is produced or inspected.

## Verification

Run `manifest_test.exs`, `runner_test.exs`, `scorer_test.exs`, and `no_fault_fixture_test.exs` under `test/elara/benchmark` with `--seed 0`, then `mix test`, format check, and warnings-as-errors compile.

## Dependencies

Blocked by frozen corpus materialization. Blocks internal and Lemon adapter-equivalence work.

### Fixture and runner evidence

* The versioned loader verifies the caller-supplied manifest checksum, every selected and unscored-adapter fixture digest/commit, row shape, and all pinned beacon artifact checksums. Malformed input and path traversal are rejected with typed diagnostics.
* Every one of the 12 selected initial fixtures was materialized, contaminated, and reset twice to its exact frozen digest.
* A target-neutral fixture adapter dry-ran all 12 no-fault tasks. All final workspace digests and frozen outcomes matched, including S03 `error_exit_7`, P04 `error_conflict`, and W08's preregistered environmental change. Ordered `task_id / initial digest / final digest / outcome` evidence SHA-256: `763bb18e63b8499561ce41b6e74925fd33a8423ca33141686dfbccca7ecf45ca`.
* Schedules are exact: 20 rows × `baseline, receipts, receipts, baseline, baseline, receipts` = 120 fault entries; 12 tasks × 2 conditions × (2 warmups + 10 measured) = 288 no-fault entries. Wrong/missing/duplicate order is invalid.
* The runner exposes one adapter callback and one barrier-identified fault hook, verifies exactly one expected hook call, records immutable metadata plus initial/final workspace, causal/noncausal observations, interventions, wall/CPU/storage, raw evidence, and reports adapter/schema failures as harness failures.

### Frozen scorer evidence

* Deliberate expected evidence scored `Pass` with `B=20`, `T=5`, `B-T=15`; the bad-recovery union is counted per consolidated row/condition, not per repetition.
* Deliberate safety evidence scored `Fail` and was not offset by aggregate value. Correct but 1.30× corpus timing evidence scored `Mixed`. A consistent `automatic_known_nonterminal` result remained valid and outside the bad union.
* Missing, skipped, non-comparable, harness-failure, inconsistent-repeat, order-drift, and zero-timing inputs each produced distinct actionable `Invalid` diagnostics.
* The scorer enforces all-repetition safe-action/knowledge convergence, applicable causal-terminal convergence, 100% no-fault correctness, exact rational medians, corpus ratio ≤ 1.20, and every task ratio ≤ 2.0.
