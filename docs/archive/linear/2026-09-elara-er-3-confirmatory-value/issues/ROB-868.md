## Description

## Outcome

At or after the frozen V6 time, fetch and cryptographically verify the exact committed drand round and deterministically materialize the fresh V6 corpus, row order, literals, fixtures, external attestation, and dogfood plan.

## Scope

Require equality from both pinned relays and official-client verification; derive the frozen V6 seed; rerun the already-frozen exhaustive preflight before sampling; select only through the frozen rule; emit fresh versioned artifacts; validate every selected task/fault/workspace/causal-terminal contract; preserve V1–V5 byte-for-byte.

## Non-goals

No alternate beacon, materializer patch/retry after selection, adapter/entrypoint repair, qualification, target/timing/comparator/dogfood run, B/T, or protocol/threshold change.

## Acceptance criteria

All outputs reproduce byte-for-byte; preflight remains 20/20 and 40/40 before selection; selected counts/membership/order and all semantic checks pass; exact beacon evidence is retained; any failure stops V6 without substitution or retry.

## Verification

Verifier and materializer twice from clean absent V6 outputs, manifest/spec/preserved-hash/no-stale-identity audits, JSON, format, compile, full tests, clean and pushed Git.
