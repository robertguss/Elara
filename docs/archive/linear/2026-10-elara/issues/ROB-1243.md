Found by ROB-1238's N=500 diagnostic (3854b7e, 2026-10-03; note 003 "Setup-stall diagnosis"; evidence lab/results/concurrency-diag/concurrency/20261003T165722555796Z-sweep-sessions-seed42/, untracked). Pre-census setup took 4 ms.

Why it matters: in this run, this interval delayed the profile's activation past the registered window, so investigating the observed harness setup delay comes before any N=500 attribution profile. That does not rule out contributions from the product's workload or from scheduling, and whether the earlier runs' delays share this localization is unproven.

Not established: queue wait vs the supervisor's own work vs scheduling vs the `Process.info/2` reads; what the 543 messages were; any link to the `:no_client` exits.

Proposed further diagnosis (one seeded run, diagnostics only): time the `which_children` call separately from each queue read; sample `Elara.SessionSup`'s `current_function`, `status` and message-queue composition (counts by request type, no payloads) at the census and periodically during load; and record SessionSup's request latencies for `start_child` and `terminate_child` during the load phase.

Untested fix direction (hypothesis only, not to be built from this issue): take the census's session enumeration off SessionSup's mailbox, for example from the coordinator's own record of the sessions it started, or move setup off the coordinator. Either needs a test that fails first, and a fix waits on the diagnosis above.
