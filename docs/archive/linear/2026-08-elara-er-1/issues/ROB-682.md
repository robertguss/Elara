## Fixed metrics

### Safety and correctness

Continue/Narrow/Pivot require:

* zero duplicate external mutation, callback reinvocation without declared atomic ID/digest mutation deduplication, wrong/corrupt effect, false-success, false-error, unsafe recommendation, or benchmark-contamination disqualifiers; and
* 100% correct no-fault final workspaces.

Any safety failure has Stop precedence.

### Internal recovery

Over the locked paired denominator, each row has one primary classification. `B` is the count of baseline rows ambiguous or requiring manual recovery, counting their union once; `T` is the same target count over identical rows.

Material improvement requires:

* `B - T >= 2`;
* `(B - T) / B >= 0.50`; and
* `B >= 2`.

Every row must converge within its frozen bound to a truthful knowledge state and safe next action: 100% required. Every row whose frozen schedule preserves preregistered admissible causal terminal evidence must converge to `completed` or `failed`: 100% required. `:indeterminate` remains valid when no causal terminal evidence can establish outcome, even if nonterminal or noncausal evidence survives.

### No-fault timing

Use two unscored warmups and 10 measured repetitions per row, per-row medians, and target/baseline ratios. Pass requires corpus median ratio at most 1.20 and no row median ratio above 2.0. CPU/storage remain reported secondary metrics.

### Dogfood

When internal safety permits execution, pass requires:

* 8–12 planned tasks all reported and at least 8 terminal classified outcomes;
* at least five supported failure types and two no-fault controls;
* zero safety disqualifiers;
* evidence-backed safe-next-action guidance for 100% of injected failures;
* at least 80% of terminal tasks without unplanned recovery intervention under the frozen denominator/rounding; and
* no task abandoned due protocol friction.

A safety-blocked [ROB-776](https://linear.app/robert-guss/issue/ROB-776/er3-4b-execute-the-frozen-dogfood-plan) implies Stop through the originating internal disqualifier; non-execution itself is not scored as dogfood failure.

### External evidence

External comparative claims require no-fault equivalence for every included operation class and at least three equivalent fault rows across two fault types. If [ROB-775](https://linear.app/robert-guss/issue/ROB-775/er3-3b-execute-the-frozen-external-runtime-comparison) is **Insufficient comparability**, make no external claim. That result alone neither supports nor blocks the internal Elara decision.

## Outcome precedence

Evaluate Stop first, then Continue, then Narrow, then Pivot. Never select a post-hoc task/row subset.

### Stop

Choose if any safety disqualifier occurs; no preregistered scope establishes material internal recovery improvement; no scope meets all-row truthful knowledge-state/safe-next-action convergence, causal-terminal-evidence convergence, or no-fault correctness; or explicit timing/dogfood thresholds fail without a qualifying Pivot contradiction.

### Continue

Choose only when the complete GATE-2-selected scope meets every applicable fixed threshold above and no unresolved deployment contradiction remains. Adopt only that proven scope and activate at most H1, only because its trigger is satisfied.

### Narrow

Choose only when the full scope fails Continue but the first preregistered strict scope in [ROB-772](https://linear.app/robert-guss/issue/ROB-772/fnd-2-preregister-evidence-thresholds-and-comparator-suitability)'s deterministic precedence meets every applicable safety, correctness, recovery, knowledge-state convergence, causal-terminal convergence, timing, and dogfood threshold. Retain exactly that scope; reject/cancel broader assumptions.

### Pivot

Choose only when safety, no-fault correctness, material recovery improvement, all-row knowledge-state convergence, causal-terminal-evidence convergence, and evidence thresholds pass, but one demonstrated ownership, UX, or persistence contradiction prevents deployment and cannot be resolved by any preregistered narrower scope. Name one bounded replacement experiment tied to exact failed rows. Pivot cannot relabel safety, insufficient value, or generic performance failure.

## Non-goals

* Fixing implementation, adapters, reports, or benchmark inputs during the gate.
* Changing denominators, thresholds, eligible scopes, comparator eligibility, or task inclusion after exposure.
* Counting unavailable external seams as advantage/disadvantage.
* Activating all horizons or creating a repository roadmap.

## Acceptance criteria

* Result begins with exactly one bold decision: **Continue**, **Narrow**, **Pivot**, or **Stop**.
* Stop precedence and every safety disqualifier are evaluated first with evidence links.
* Internal paired, external, and dogfood evidence are reported separately before synthesis.
* `B`, `T`, absolute/relative improvement, all-row knowledge-state denominator, causal-terminal-evidence denominator, timing aggregation, dogfood denominators, and every threshold have explicit pass/fail.
* External report below the floor is labeled Insufficient comparability and produces no comparative claim.
* Narrow, if selected, proves it is the first passing preregistered strict scope; Pivot names exact rows and one contradiction.
* Decision states exact identity, receipt, operation, postcondition, failover, workspace, authority, and evidence assumptions that survive.
* Executor completed remains distinct from postcondition_satisfied.
* Result contains one falsifiable final thesis and explicit rejected broader/BEAM-superiority claims.
* Linear is updated consistently: adopted scope/project status, cancellations, native blockers, one next Todo maximum, and at most one activated horizon.
