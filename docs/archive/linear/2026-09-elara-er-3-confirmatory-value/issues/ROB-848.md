## Frozen v3 corrections

* Change P05 secondary fault from F3 to F4 and P06 secondary fault from F3 to F2 before seed selection.
* Add a mechanical compatibility predicate over every candidate task/primary/secondary fault before committing the future seed: F3 requires exactly one fault-target job mutation before terminal delivery, no environmental mutation substituted for it, and a reachable post-cut recovery/workspace contract. Multi-step tasks must separate fault-target postcondition from complete task workspace and freeze whether continuation runs after each recovered outcome.

* Preserve the Universal scope, candidate membership, target commits, thresholds, formulas, Lemon pin, dogfood frame, and runtime-neutral thesis unless the compatibility audit proves another explicit pre-seed correction necessary.
* Commit a genuinely future drand mainnet round and a versioned v3 seed derivation before its nominal time. Preserve all v1/v2 files byte-for-byte.
