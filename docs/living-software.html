01 · Thesis

Not a finished artifact. A governed living system.

Software continuously receives signals from people, agents, production, dependencies, policy, markets, and its environment; interprets contradictions; and adapts while preserving identity and obligations. The opportunity is an epistemic and causal operating system for changing complex systems.

Code remains consequential, but may become a materialized projection of intent, constraints, evidence, platform capabilities, and current reality. The durable identity of an application moves toward the obligations it preserves—not one arrangement of source files.

02 · Constitutional compiler

A language above implementation

The constitution describes what a system is for, what it promises, what it must never do, who may change it, and what evidence is sufficient. It combines human-legible values with typed obligations, formal cores, executable policy, examples, precedent, and explicit judgment.

Purpose

Values and outcomes

Affected people, desired outcomes, non-goals, and tradeoffs remain human-legible and contestable.

Promises

Obligations and invariants

Durable, temporal promises plus safety, compatibility, privacy, security, and architecture constraints.

Power

Authority and adaptation

Capability-based rules govern who may observe, decide, amend, execute, and approve—and what may adapt autonomously.

Truth

Evidence and amendment

Risk-sensitive proof policies, retained dissent, expiration, precedent, and explicit constitutional evolution.

Four forms of semantics

Normative

What ought to be true?

Purpose, values, obligations, protected people, and unacceptable outcomes.

Operational

What may act?

Permitted, forbidden, and conditional actions with required capabilities.

Evidentiary

What justifies belief?

Evidence scope, assumptions, contradictions, sufficiency, and invalidation.

Evolutionary

What may change?

Amendment, migration, dissent, expiry, and the lawful evolution of authority.

Compiler and continuous controller

Compilation is not one-way. A constitutional controller continuously compares desired obligations with scoped evidence about reality, then asks what changed, what authority exists, and which bounded Mission could safely reduce the difference.

03 · Epistemic operating system

Schedule belief, attention, authority, and risk

Durability

Obligations, not tickets

Promises survive agents, processes, environments, branches, releases, and handoffs. Missions serve obligations; evidence determines satisfaction.

Knowledge

Epistemic types

Observed, measured, reproduced, derived, proven, inferred, contradicted, unknown, and indeterminate claims retain source, scope, assumptions, and invalidation.

Security

Context as capability

Information arrives with provenance, trust, allowed uses, privacy, validity, contamination, and a capability ceiling.

Scarcity

Attention scheduler

More computation resolves reducible uncertainty; people receive irreducible ambiguity, ethics, risk, and value judgment.

Effects

Privileged syscalls

Every consequential mutation has authority, stable identity, durable acceptance, an honest outcome, and reconciliation.

Autonomy

Reversibility policy

Authority grows with containment, observability, reversibility, and bounded blast radius—not faith in model perfection.

05 · Constitutional laws

Ten invariants for the platform

No work without whyEvery Mission traces to an obligation, observation, or bounded exploration.
No effect without authorityConsequential mutations require capabilities and policy.
No effect without a receiptOutcomes include honest uncertainty and reconciliation.
No accepted claim without evidenceWeak support must remain visibly weak.
No evidence without scopeProof names where, when, and under which assumptions it applies.
No artifact without lineageOutputs identify what produced them and why.
No autonomy without containmentAuthority scales with reversibility, observability, and blast radius.
No memory without invalidationKnowledge that cannot become stale becomes dangerous.
No metric without gaming analysisAssume agents and institutions optimize what is measured.
No self-modification without governanceThe system cannot redefine success and certify itself.

The dominant danger is false certainty at scale

Correlated agents can inherit one bad assumption, reinforce it through generated knowledge, satisfy completion metrics, and suppress dissent through apparent consensus. Unknowns, contradictions, failed predictions, and minority hypotheses must remain first-class.

07 · Honest boundaries

Where the metaphor breaks

The analogy illuminates

Homeostasis preserves invariants. Metabolism removes expired structure. An immune system detects threats and contradictions. Senses observe reality. Memory preserves adaptation. Evolution explores alternatives.

The analogy can mislead

Organisms optimize survival and reproduction. Software must remain subordinate to human purpose. It must not preserve itself, expand its authority, suppress shutdown, or rewrite values merely to fit observed behavior.

Implementation details do not become irrelevant. Fewer humans may author them, but details remain where security, performance, correctness, cost, and failure physically occur. Generated implementations must be disposable and regenerable without becoming opaque. Humans retain drill-down, explanations, reproducibility, and escape hatches.

08 · Falsifiable path

Discover semantics before syntax

Do not begin with a beautiful DSL. First exercise purpose, obligations, invariants, claims, evidence, capabilities, effects, Missions, amendments, precedent, and contradictions using Markdown or simple data structures. Syntax should crystallize concepts that survive contact with real work.

Ten experiments

  1. Mission envelope: typed objective, capability, deliverable, and evidence policy.
  2. Evidence result: separate claims, proof, artifacts, effects, and uncertainty.
  3. Tiny constitution: model plugin hot reloading—preserve session history, coherent generations, atomic activation, reload rollback, authority, and compatibility evidence—then ask Harness to interpret and test it.
  4. Bidirectional contradiction: turn production evidence into a Mission or amendment proposal.
  5. Proof invalidation: expire only evidence touched by one changed assumption.
  6. Diverse verification: compare correlated consensus with blind and deterministic review.
  7. Effect receipt: crash at every external-mutation boundary.
  8. Semantic deletion: remove an artifact whose obligation expired.
  9. Prediction-bearing release: evaluate declared predictions against reality.
  10. Constitutional self-hosting: replay Harness history under a proposed runtime change.
Humans govern purpose, values, legitimacy, and irreducible judgment. Agents interpret bounded Missions and handle implementation. Verification maintains justified belief. Production supplies reality. The system adapts through explicit evidence and amendments while preserving authority, causality, reversibility, and the right to stop.

North star: trusted adaptation per unit of scarce human attention.
