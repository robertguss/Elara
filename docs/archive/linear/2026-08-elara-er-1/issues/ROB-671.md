## Description

## Outcome

Execute and preserve the complete deterministic ER-1 falsification matrix: eight fully specified crash rows plus normal, same-digest replay, and conflicting-digest controls. Distinguish durable controller/executor truth, workspace observation, historical execution knowledge, and session classification without timing races.

## Context

[ROB-667](https://linear.app/robert-guss/issue/ROB-667/fnd-1-freeze-the-er-1-contract-and-baseline-current-behavior) freezes each row's crash target, failpoint, messages, storage survival, restart order, admissible evidence, expected classification/action, deadline, and convergence bound. [ROB-670](https://linear.app/robert-guss/issue/ROB-670/er1-3-connect-one-marker-mutation-to-acceptance-and-reconciliation) supplies the integrated marker path. This issue executes that frozen contract; it must expose contradictions rather than edit expectations after results.

## Crash rows

For each row, apply the exact [ROB-667](https://linear.app/robert-guss/issue/ROB-667/fnd-1-freeze-the-er-1-contract-and-baseline-current-behavior) schedule, restart the named owners/storage in the frozen order, reconcile only from admissible evidence, and assert final facts plus marker count:

1. before controller intent commit;
2. after intent commit and before dispatch;
3. after executor receipt and before durable acceptance commit;
4. after acceptance commit and before acceptance reply;
5. after acceptance reply and before callback invocation/external mutation;
6. after external mutation and before executor completion commit;
7. after completion commit and before completion reply;
8. after completion reply and before session-result persistence.

Row 6 must apply the frozen causal-evidence rule: surviving `accepted` alone never becomes terminal; a non-deduplicated callback is not retried; postcondition observation alone cannot create `completed`; and only an atomic exclusive job-ID/digest-bound causal receipt may support terminal reconciliation. Otherwise assert bounded `:indeterminate` with all observations and safe no-retry action.

The observed table records:

* crash target/failpoint and delivered/dropped messages;
* surviving controller/executor storage and restart order;
* durable controller state and last proven executor fact;
* admission count, callback-attempt count, and external marker mutation count/digest;
* historical-execution knowledge versus current postcondition observation;
* session-visible outcome;
* permitted action actually taken;
* automatic convergence, attempts, and elapsed time against the frozen bound;
* exact missing causal terminal evidence plus every surviving nonterminal or noncausal observation for each indeterminate row.

## Controls

Execute and repeat:

1. no-fault one admission, one callback attempt, exactly one external marker mutation, and one terminal result;
2. same-ID/same-digest submit/query without a new admission, callback attempt, or external mutation;
3. same-ID/conflicting-digest rejection without mutation.

## Non-goals

* Changing any frozen row, admissible evidence source, expected classification, or convergence bound.
* Adding recovery behavior while running the matrix.
* Real write/patch/shell support, random killing, load testing, broad property infrastructure, or generic exactly-once callback claims; the safety claim is one admission and no duplicate external mutation.
* Hiding failed, slow, or permanently indeterminate rows.

* All eight crash rows and three controls are independently named and deterministically reproducible.
* Observed crash target, messages, surviving stores, restart order, and admissible evidence match [ROB-667](https://linear.app/robert-guss/issue/ROB-667/fnd-1-freeze-the-er-1-contract-and-baseline-current-behavior) exactly.
* Normal execution creates one admission/callback attempt/external marker mutation/result; every row asserts admission, callback-attempt, and external-mutation counts separately, not marker existence alone.
* Same-ID/same-digest submit/query creates no second admission or callback attempt; conflicting digest never executes; an accepted callback is never reinvoked without declared atomic ID/digest mutation deduplication.
* Every row converges within its frozen bound to a truthful knowledge state and safe next action; `completed`/`failed` convergence is required wherever admissible causal terminal evidence survives.
* A row may be indeterminate when no admissible causal terminal evidence establishes completion/failure; it names that missing fact and all surviving intent, acceptance, callback-attempt, external-mutation, and postcondition evidence.
* Workspace or postcondition evidence never fabricates executor `completed`; durable causal completion, historical execution observations, current postcondition, and session classification are reported independently.
* Executor completed and postcondition_satisfied are reported separately.
* No accepted job executes on a replacement executor; transcript repair supplies no effect truth.
* Any failed safety/control/truth/liveness assertion remains visible and flows unchanged to GATE-1.
* Result links every observed row/control to exact tests and captured evidence.

### Deviations and remaining uncertainty

The frozen expectations were not changed. The matrix exposed two omitted [ROB-670](https://linear.app/robert-guss/issue/ROB-670/er1-3-connect-one-marker-mutation-to-acceptance-and-reconciliation) integration behaviors before evidence could be collected: no-intent reopen incorrectly fell through to generic `interrupted`, and a live controller finalized pre-attempt executor transport loss instead of awaiting the same owner's reopen. This commit adds truthful `not_started`, a pending-effect shell state, executor monitoring/reconnection, and evidence-driven reconciliation. That is a **procedural deviation** from [ROB-671](https://linear.app/robert-guss/issue/ROB-671/er1-4-execute-the-eight-cut-deterministic-crash-matrix)'s non-goal of adding recovery behavior while running the matrix, and is preserved here for GATE-1 review rather than hidden. A control-only barrier after accepted observation was also added to inspect replay behavior while the executor is available. No frozen row, evidence source, expected classification, or deadline changed.

The live reconnect API is test-only and explicitly supplied by the matrix; ER-1 does not prove production discovery/supervision of a restarted executor, distributed ownership, fencing, cancellation, or generic exactly-once effects.
